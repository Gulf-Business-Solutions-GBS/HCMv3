## Overview

A share of the employee's cost assigned for a period, under up to seven cost elements.

It is recorded for reference; the cost engine and journal postings do not read it at present. Use Employee Wage Accounts for account mapping that postings use.

## CostPercentage {#EmployeeAccountAssignment.CostPercentage}
<!-- alias: loc:CostPercentage, dom:EmployeeAccountAssignment_CostPercentage -->

**What it is.** The percentage of cost assigned.

**Example.** 50

**Rules.** Optional. Number.

## CostElement1Id {#EmployeeAccountAssignment.CostElement1Id}
<!-- alias: loc:CostElement1Id, dom:EmployeeAccountAssignment_costElement1Id -->

**What it is.** Cost element 1 -- one of up to seven accounting dimensions.

**Why it matters.** Recorded for reference; the cost engine and journal postings do not read it at present.

**Rules.** Optional. Chosen from Cost Element master data.
