## Overview

A skill on a CV.

## Skill {#CVSkill.Skill}
<!-- alias: loc:Skill, dom:CVSkill_Skill -->

**What it is.** The skill.

**Example.** Financial reporting

**Rules.** Required. Maximum 256 characters.

## Proficiency {#CVSkill.Proficiency}
<!-- alias: loc:Proficiency, dom:CVSkill_Proficiency -->

**What it is.** How good the candidate is at it.

**Example.** Advanced

**Rules.** Required. One of: Novice, Intermediate, Advanced.
