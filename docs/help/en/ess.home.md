## Overview

The front page of self service: what is waiting on you, what you are waiting on, everything you can ask for, and what you asked for recently.

What appears here follows your company and your role. A tile you cannot see is a transaction your company has not switched on, or one your permissions do not include — not something missing.

## Waiting on me {#ess.home.waiting-on-me}
<!-- alias: loc:WaitingOnMe -->

**What it is.** How many requests are sitting in your inbox for a decision — your team's, and anything delegated to you.

**Example.** 4 waiting.

**Why it matters.** Every one of these is somebody held up until you look at it. It is the one number on this page that costs other people time while you leave it.

## My requests awaiting approval {#ess.home.awaiting-approval}
<!-- alias: loc:MyRequestsAwaitingApproval -->

**What it is.** How many of your own requests are still waiting on somebody else.

**Example.** 2 of yours out with approvers.

**Why it matters.** These are yours, not decisions for you to make. Where one has been out too long, the request's workflow history says who has it, which is who to ask.

## What would you like to do {#ess.home.tiles}
<!-- alias: loc:WhatWouldYouLikeToDo -->

**What it is.** A tile for each thing you can ask for — leave, a loan, a ticket, a letter — showing only what your company has switched on and your permissions allow.

**Example.** Tiles for leave, tickets and letters, but none for travel at a company that does not use it.

**Why it matters.** It is the whole of what self service can do for you, in one place. If something you expect is not here, it is a permission or a configuration question for HR, not something to look for elsewhere in the menu.

## No approval needed {#ess.home.no-approval-needed}
<!-- alias: loc:NoApprovalNeeded -->

**What it is.** A note on a tile whose requests take effect as soon as you submit them, because your company has set no approval for that transaction.

**Example.** Recording a document, where HR has decided it needs no sign-off.

**Why it matters.** There is no approver to catch a mistake, and nothing to withdraw before somebody sees it. Check what you have typed before you save on those.

## My recent requests {#ess.home.recent-requests}
<!-- alias: loc:MyRecentRequests -->

**What it is.** The last few things you asked for, of any kind, with where each has got to.

**Example.** Leave approved on Sunday; a ticket claim still pending.

**Why it matters.** It is the quick answer to "did that go through?" across every screen at once, without opening each one. For the full list of one kind, go to its own screen.

## My team {#ess.home.my-team}
<!-- alias: loc:EssMyTeam -->

**What it is.** A mark shown to people who have others reporting to them, alongside what their team needs from them.

**Example.** Shown to a line manager; not shown to somebody with no reports.

**Why it matters.** It is how you tell approvals that are yours as a manager from requests that are yours as an employee. If you manage people and do not see it, your reporting line is not recorded — which also means your team's requests are not reaching you.
