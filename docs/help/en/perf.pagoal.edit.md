## Overview

A goal written on a PA Form. Part of the older period-based appraisal form (PA Form). Stored with the form; no report reads it at present.

## Goal {#PAGoal.Goal}
<!-- alias: loc:Goal, dom:PAGoal_Goal -->

**What it is.** The goal, in words.

**Example.** Cut month-end close to five days

**Rules.** Optional. Maximum 2000 characters.
