## Overview

Start the clearance you need before leaving — at the end of your employment, before long leave, or when you move between departments — and answer the checklist lines that are yours.

Two things happen on this screen. You raise your own clearance and watch it go round; and where you are the person a department relies on — IT, Finance, Stores — the lines other people's clearances put to you turn up here for you to answer.

## Clearance type {#ess.clearance.clearance-type}
<!-- alias: loc:ClearanceType -->

**What it is.** Why you are clearing — end of service, long leave, a transfer between departments.

**Example.** End of Service Clearance.

**Why it matters.** The type decides the whole checklist: who has to sign off and what they check. An end-of-service clearance asks far more of you than one for a transfer, and picking the wrong one sends your clearance to the wrong people.

## Start date {#ess.clearance.start-date}
<!-- alias: loc:StartDate -->

**What it is.** When the clearance begins.

**Example.** Two weeks before your last day, so there is time to clear it.

**Why it matters.** Clearance takes as long as its slowest department. Starting it on your last day is how people end up waiting for a final payment after they have gone.

## End date {#ess.clearance.end-date}
<!-- alias: loc:EndDate -->

**What it is.** The day it needs to be finished by — usually your last working day.

**Example.** 31 October 2026.

**Why it matters.** It is the date the departments work to, and for an end-of-service clearance it is what the final settlement waits on. A clearance still open on this date holds up the payment.

## Clearance checklist {#ess.clearance.checklist}
<!-- alias: loc:ClearanceChecklist -->

**What it is.** The lines a clearance is made of, one per thing to confirm, each belonging to a department. Some are yours to answer.

**Example.** "Laptop returned" — IT; "No outstanding loan" — Finance; "Locker emptied" — Admin.

**Why it matters.** A clearance finishes when every line is answered, so a single unanswered line holds the whole thing. If a line is with you and you cannot answer it yet, saying so in the comments is better than leaving it — nobody can see why it is stuck otherwise.
