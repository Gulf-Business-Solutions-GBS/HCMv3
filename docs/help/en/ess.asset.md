## Overview

Ask for a company asset — a laptop, a phone, a vehicle, a SIM — and see what is already signed out to you.

An approved request puts the asset on your name. It stays there until it is handed back and returned in the system, and anything still on your name at the end of your employment turns up on your clearance, so the list here is worth keeping true.

## Asset {#ess.asset.asset}
<!-- alias: loc:Asset -->

**What it is.** The item you are asking for, from what your company has on its register and free to give out.

**Example.** Laptop — Dell Latitude, tag IT-00432.

**Why it matters.** You are asking for that particular item, not for one of its kind. If the one you want is not in the list it is already out with somebody or not registered, and that is a question for whoever keeps the register.

## Start date {#ess.asset.start-date}
<!-- alias: loc:StartDate -->

**What it is.** When you need it from.

**Example.** 1 September 2026.

**Why it matters.** It is the date the asset is booked to you, so it decides whether anybody else can have it in the meantime — and a start date earlier than today does not backdate the handover, it only makes the record wrong.

## End date {#ess.asset.end-date}
<!-- alias: loc:EndDate -->

**What it is.** When you expect to hand it back. Leave it blank for something you hold for as long as you are here.

**Example.** 30 September 2026 for a projector borrowed for a month; blank for your own laptop.

**Why it matters.** A date that has passed does not return the asset on its own — somebody still has to take it back and record it. What the date does is tell them when to expect it, and put the item back into circulation for planning.
