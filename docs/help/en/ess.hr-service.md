## Overview

Ask HR for one of the services your company offers that does not have a screen of its own — renewing a residency, a driving licence transfer, a medical check, a civil ID errand.

It is the catch-all: your company decides which services exist, and what each one costs and involves. Where a service has a fee, the request also records what is being paid and by whom.

## Service {#ess.hr-service.hr-service-type}
<!-- alias: loc:HRServiceType -->

**What it is.** Which service you are asking for, from the list your company maintains.

**Example.** Residency Renewal; Driving Licence Transfer; Medical Examination.

**Why it matters.** Every service has its own owner in HR and its own turnaround, and several of them have a government deadline behind them. If what you need is not in the list, it is not a service your company runs through this screen — ask HR directly.

## Service date {#ess.hr-service.service-date}
<!-- alias: loc:ServiceDate -->

**What it is.** When you need it done by, or the date it applies to.

**Example.** 15 October 2026 — the day your residency expires.

**Why it matters.** For anything with a government deadline this is the date that matters, and HR works to it. Give the real deadline, not the date you would like it done, so the queue is ordered by what is actually urgent.

## Amount {#ess.hr-service.amount}
<!-- alias: loc:Amount -->

**What it is.** The fee involved, where the service has one.

**Example.** 10.000 for a licence transfer.

**Why it matters.** It is what the charge is settled against afterwards — deducted from your pay, or borne by the company, according to your company's rule for that service. Leave it blank when you do not know the fee; HR fills in what it actually came to.
