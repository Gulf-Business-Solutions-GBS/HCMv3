## Overview

Claim back what you spent on an approved business trip — taxis, hotel extras, meals, anything your company's travel policy covers and did not pay directly.

This is the claim, not the trip. The trip itself is requested and approved under **My Travel**, and the allowance your company pays per day is handled there; what you claim here is money out of your own pocket.

## Claim date {#ess.travel-claim.claim-date}
<!-- alias: loc:ClaimDate -->

**What it is.** The day the money was spent.

**Example.** 14 May 2026 — the taxi from the airport.

**Why it matters.** It has to fall inside a trip that was approved, which is what an approver checks first. It also decides which payroll period reimburses you.

## Claim amount {#ess.travel-claim.claim-amount}
<!-- alias: loc:ClaimAmount -->

**What it is.** How much you are claiming, in your payroll currency.

**Example.** 27.500

**Why it matters.** Claim what you actually spent, converted at the rate you actually got. Keep the receipt — most companies want to see it before paying, and a claim without one is the first thing an approver queries.
