## Overview

Hand your approvals to a colleague while you are away, so requests waiting on you do not sit there until you are back.

A delegation moves the approving, not the responsibility. Everything your delegate approves is recorded as approved by them on your behalf, and the workflow history says so — which is exactly what you want it to say when you look back at it.

## Delegate to {#ess.delegation.delegate-to}
<!-- alias: loc:DelegateTo -->

**What it is.** The colleague who will act for you. The list holds the people your company allows you to delegate to.

**Example.** Your deputy, or the person who covers your team.

**Why it matters.** Choose someone senior enough for what will land on them: they will be approving things in your place, and they cannot pass them on again. If the person you want is not in the list, your company does not allow that delegation and HR is who to ask.

## Start date {#ess.delegation.start-date}
<!-- alias: loc:StartDate -->

**What it is.** The first day your delegate acts for you.

**Example.** 1 August 2026, the day your leave begins.

**Why it matters.** Nothing moves before this date, so a delegation starting the day after you fly leaves one day of requests sitting with you and nobody able to touch them.

## End date {#ess.delegation.end-date}
<!-- alias: loc:EndDate -->

**What it is.** The last day it applies. After it, approvals come back to you on their own.

**Example.** 14 August 2026.

**Why it matters.** It is what stops the delegation — there is nothing to switch off afterwards. Set it to the day you are actually back at work, not the day you land.

## Currently active {#ess.delegation.currently-active}
<!-- alias: loc:CurrentlyActive -->

**What it is.** Whether this delegation is in force today, from its dates and its approval.

**Example.** Approved last week for next month — not active yet.

**Why it matters.** Approved and active are different things. This column is the one to read before you travel: if it does not say your delegation is active on the days you are away, your approvals are not going anywhere.
