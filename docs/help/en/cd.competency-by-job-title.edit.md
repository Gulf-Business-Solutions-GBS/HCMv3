## Overview

The competencies expected of a job title, for a date range. The end date cannot be before the start date. Kept for reference: it is not copied into employees' forms.

## JobTitle {#CompetencyByJobTitle.JobTitleId}
<!-- alias: loc:JobTitle, dom:CompetencyByJobTitle_JobTitleId -->

**What it is.** The job title.

**Example.** Sales Executive

**Rules.** Required. Chosen from Job Title master data.
