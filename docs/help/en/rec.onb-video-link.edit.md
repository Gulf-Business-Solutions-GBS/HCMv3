## Overview

A link to an onboarding video.

## Link {#OnbVideoLink.Link}
<!-- alias: loc:Link, dom:OnbVideoLink_Link -->

**What it is.** The video's address.

**Example.** `https://…/welcome.mp4`

**Rules.** Optional. Maximum 500 characters.

## IsActive {#OnbVideoLink.IsActive}
<!-- alias: loc:IsActive, dom:OnbVideoLink_IsActive -->

**What it is.** Whether the link is in use.

**Rules.** Yes or no.
