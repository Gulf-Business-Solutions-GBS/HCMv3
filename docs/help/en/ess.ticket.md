## Overview

Claim an air ticket your contract entitles you to — yours, or your family's where the entitlement covers them. You say which entitlement you are claiming and when you are travelling; HR and payroll do the rest.

Nothing is booked from here. An approved request records the entitlement as taken and, depending on your company, either pays you the allowance through payroll or hands the booking to whoever arranges travel.

## Ticket type {#ess.ticket.ticket-type}
<!-- alias: loc:TicketType -->

**What it is.** Which entitlement you are claiming: the annual ticket, a repatriation ticket at end of service, an emergency ticket, and so on. The list holds the types your company has set up.

**Example.** Annual Leave Ticket.

**Why it matters.** The type decides who is covered, how often it can be claimed and what it is worth, so claiming the wrong one is the usual reason a request comes back rejected.

## Ticket date {#ess.ticket.ticket-date}
<!-- alias: loc:TicketDate -->

**What it is.** When you are travelling.

**Example.** 12 July 2026.

**Why it matters.** It decides which entitlement year the claim falls in, so a date either side of your anniversary can be the difference between a claim you have and one you have already used.

## Amount {#ess.ticket.amount}
<!-- alias: loc:Amount -->

**What it is.** What you are claiming, in your payroll currency. Leaving it blank claims the entitlement and lets payroll price it.

**Example.** 180.000 — or blank, which is the usual way.

**Why it matters.** Blank is safer: payroll then applies your company's own rate for that ticket type, and you cannot get it wrong. Put a figure in only where you are claiming against something you actually paid.
