## Overview

Ask HR for a letter — a salary certificate, an employment letter, a no-objection letter for a visa or a driving licence — and follow it until it is ready to collect.

The letter is produced from your own record, so nothing is typed by hand and the figures on it are the ones the system holds. What you supply is who it is addressed to.

## Letter type {#ess.letter.document-type}
<!-- alias: loc:DocumentType -->

**What it is.** Which letter you need, from the templates your company has.

**Example.** Salary Certificate; Employment Letter; No Objection Certificate.

**Why it matters.** The type decides what the letter says and how much of it — a salary certificate states your pay, an employment letter usually does not. Whoever asked you for the letter will have told you which one they need.

## Effective date {#ess.letter.effective-date}
<!-- alias: loc:EffectiveDate -->

**What it is.** The date the letter should carry.

**Example.** Today's date, for a letter the bank wants now.

**Why it matters.** Most people asking for a letter will not take one dated more than a month or so ago, and the details on it are taken as at this date — so for a current letter, leave it at today.

## Addressed to (English) {#ess.letter.address-to-en}
<!-- alias: loc:AddressToEn -->

**What it is.** Who the letter is for, in English. Required.

**Example.** Gulf Bank — Salmiya Branch.

**Why it matters.** It is printed on the letter exactly as you type it, and a letter addressed to the wrong body is usually refused by the body it was meant for. Write the organisation's own name, not "To whom it may concern", unless that really is what you were asked for.

## Addressed to (Arabic) {#ess.letter.address-to-ar}
<!-- alias: loc:AddressToAr -->

**What it is.** The same, in Arabic, for a letter that will be issued in Arabic.

**Example.** بنك الخليج — فرع السالمية

**Why it matters.** Government bodies and most local banks want the Arabic letter, and they want their own name as they write it. Copy it from something they issued rather than translating it yourself.

## Ready to collect {#ess.letter.ready-to-collect}
<!-- alias: loc:ReadyToCollect -->

**What it is.** A mark on an approved request saying HR has produced the letter and it is waiting for you.

**Example.** Approved on Sunday, ready to collect on Monday.

**Why it matters.** Approved and ready are not the same thing. Approved means HR agreed to write it; this mark means it exists — which is when going to fetch it is worth the walk.
